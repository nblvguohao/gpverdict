GPverdict

Choose genomic prediction methods for selection — from your own cross-validation, in your browser.

Which metric can rank methods?

Checked on your predictions: only metrics that ignore order-preserving changes and respond to order reversal represent within-environment selection.

Which method, and is its lead real?

Methods ranked by the within-environment rank and Pearson correlations, with the methods your trial cannot tell apart from the leaders.

What would RMSE have chosen?

The method an error-magnitude ranking picks, and an out-of-sample test of how much selection gain each rule recovers.

How large must a trial be?

Genotype–environment cells needed to resolve a given accuracy gain.

One row per environment, genotype and method, with columns environment, genotype, observed, predicted, method (also accepted: Env, k, y, p). Predictions should come from cross-validation.

Loading Python in your browser…

Your data stay on this computer: every computation runs locally in the browser (Pyodide); nothing is uploaded.

Forward evaluation over target years

Predictions of each target year made from earlier years only. Each method is compared with a reference method within environments, the differences are pooled over target years (DerSimonian–Laird with Hartung–Knapp alongside), and a difference counts as resolved only if it is at least 3/√N and its interval excludes zero (Lv and Gu 2026, forward benchmark, manuscript in preparation).

Columns environment, year, genotype, observed, predicted, method; optional dataset, and new (1 = new line) or first_year to report new and old lines separately.
