GPverdict

Choose genomic prediction methods for selection — from your own cross-validation, in your browser.

Which metric can rank methods?

Checked on your predictions: only metrics that ignore order-preserving changes and respond to order reversal represent within-environment selection.

Which method, and is its lead real?

Methods ranked by the admissible metric, with the methods your trial cannot tell apart from the leader.

What would RMSE have chosen?

The method an error-magnitude ranking picks, and an out-of-sample test of how much selection gain each rule recovers.

How large must a trial be?

Genotype–environment cells needed to resolve a given accuracy gain.

One row per environment, genotype and method, with columns environment, genotype, observed, predicted, method (also accepted: Env, k, y, p). Predictions should come from cross-validation.
